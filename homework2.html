Healthy Foods

Why Fruits Are Important

Fruits provide vitamins, minerals, and other nutrients that help keep our bodies healthy.

Healthy Eating

A balanced diet should include fruits, vegetables, grains, and other nutritious foods.

Tip: Drink plenty of water every day!

Nutrition Tips

  • Eat fresh fruits and vegetables.
  • Drink enough water.
  • Limit sugary foods.
  • Eat a balanced diet.
